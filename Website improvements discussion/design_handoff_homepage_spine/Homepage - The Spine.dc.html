Philosophy

Why “Liminal”?

Liminal means threshold — the space of crossing from one side to another.

Learning a language isn't really about vocabulary or grammar. It's a door. On the other side of that door is a culture, a way of thinking, relationships and experiences you simply cannot access any other way.

The journey

I did it. We'll do it together.

I made the jump myself — from classroom methods to learning Japanese through immersion, up to JLPT N2 (roughly CEFR B2). I never considered myself a “gifted” learner: what got me there was consistency, not talent. I'm not observing your journey from the outside — I'm on the same road, a few steps ahead.
